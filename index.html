About DAPP Consultants

Engineering knowledge. Construction delivery. Project control.

DAPP Consultants is a Melbourne-based civil construction and project management consultancy. We bring together civil engineering knowledge, construction delivery experience and project management capability so that clients have one team that understands both the design intent and the realities of building it.

Our approach is practical and commercially aware. We plan the work properly, communicate clearly and keep programme, quality, safety and cost in focus from tender through to handover.

  • Civil engineering understanding applied on site
  • Construction delivery and supervision capability
  • Structured project management and reporting
